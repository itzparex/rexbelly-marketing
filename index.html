How I'd build a customer marketing engine

Repeat revenue comes from systems that run every day, not one-off campaigns. Here's how I'd apply that to a B2B customer base.

Expansion

Partner with Customer Success, Sales and RevOps to find accounts showing expansion signals. Run targeted, always-on programs for cross-sell and new capabilities, measured through to pipeline.

Customer proof

Start from the evidence Sales needs to win. Find customers with strong results and turn them into stories, quotes and data points that are easy to find and use.

Advocacy

Build a healthy pool of willing advocates with Customer Success, and match the right customer to each request without leaning on the same few names.

My background is consumer e-commerce. The core work carries over: find the right segments, give them a relevant reason to buy more, and measure what it earns.
